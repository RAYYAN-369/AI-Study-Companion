The pipeline

Five steps, one retrieval-augmented plan

Every study plan is grounded in what you actually uploaded — the model retrieves the relevant chunks before it writes a single line of the plan.

  1. 01

    Upload

    Notes or syllabus — PDF, TXT, or DOCX.

  2. 02

    Chunk

    Text is split into overlapping, retrievable passages.

  3. 03

    Embed & store

    Each chunk becomes a vector, stored in Qdrant.

  4. 04

    Retrieve

    Your topic pulls back the most relevant passages.

  5. 05

    Generate plan

    An LLM turns retrieved context into a structured plan.

About this project

Built as a Week 3 project for the AI / GenAI Fellowship Program. Study Companion is a retrieval-augmented generation pipeline: your notes are chunked, embedded, and stored in Qdrant, then retrieved against the topic you're studying to generate a structured plan. It's guidance for a study session — not a replacement for reading the source material.
